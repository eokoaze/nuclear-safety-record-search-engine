SEQUOYAH NUCLEAR PLANT – NOTIFICATION OF INSPECTION AND REQUEST FOR 
INFORMATION 

Ricardo Medina: 

From February 3 – 7, 2025, the Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) will perform an 
inspection of the Independent Spent Fuel Storage Installation at the Sequoyah Nuclear Plant, in 
accordance with NRC inspection procedure IP 60855, “Operation of An Independent Spent Fuel 
Storage Installation.”
